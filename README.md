# Mountain Climbing Gallery

This public gallery holds images captured from the real Roblox experience after privacy and rights review. Construction-progress images are labeled as such and never presented as final realism or Play evidence. The public page is live, while its current browser interaction and broader page requirements remain unfinished.

## Open the gallery

Open the [public gallery](https://ding-ding-projects.github.io/mountain-climbing-roblox-gallery/). The current source candidate has eleven reviewed construction-progress records. Public delivery is verified only for the seven-image `main` revision in [live-publication-007](docs/evidence/live-publication-007.json), source revision `fcbb06c86738f3196340435cfe9b8deda8856494`; each of those seven image responses matched its manifest SHA-256. The four new Bakery images are not yet hosted or delivery-verified. Earlier receipts remain available. The page uses no remote scripts, fonts, images, analytics, or runtime services.

## Evidence policy

Each image record includes a content SHA-256, source state, viewport, capture method, privacy and rights review, and a capture time only when a validated receipt provides it. Unavailable scale, theme, or timestamp values remain explicitly unavailable. See [the evidence guide](docs/evidence/README.md).

## Current status

The Bakery pilot is applied in Studio version 99 with 1,013 parts, seven supplied doors, and 20 markers. The original 538-part interior, earlier 986-part pilot, and 4,003 supplied-original descendants remain preserved. Play started in 10.066 seconds; the runtime binder was ready in 5.520 seconds. One 6.015-second client sample measured 60.012 FPS, which is not broad performance acceptance. The pilot remains unaccepted: room clearance, individual doors, privacy, service behavior, and save/reopen remain unverified. Four new scene-placed Play-mode images show only the project-built side wall, roof, chimney, counter, bread, oven, and signs. They document appearance only, not physical entry, room circulation, collision, service operation, persistence, realism, or facility acceptance. Their exact capture time and display scale are unavailable; camera preparation times are not capture times. See the [current progress evidence](docs/evidence/progress-version99-20260929.json) and the per-image limitations in `docs/evidence/gallery.json`. The seven-image hosted delivery remains independently verified at `docs/evidence/live-publication-007.json`; the four new images are source-only pending delivery verification. Browser interaction and the remaining page contract are still separate unfinished work.
